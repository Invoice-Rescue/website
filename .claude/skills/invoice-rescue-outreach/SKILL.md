# Invoice Rescue — B2B Outreach & Copy Generation Skill

## Purpose

Use this skill when drafting cold email outreach, follow-up cadences, LinkedIn messages, or audit deliverables for prospective Invoice Rescue clients.

## Core Value Proposition

- **Positioning**: AI-assisted credit control for UK service businesses. Every overdue invoice chased in the client's name, escalating and professional, until it is paid.
- **Differentiator**: Unlike automated SaaS competitors (Chaser, Satago), every AI-drafted message is reviewed and approved by a human operator before sending.
- **Pricing Tiers**:
  - **Foundation (£349/mo)**: Up to 15 invoices chased/mo. First-line reminders & follow-ups.
  - **Engine (£649/mo)**: Up to 40 invoices chased/mo. Full 3-step escalation sequence + statutory interest calculation + Friday cash reports.
  - **Operator (£1,250/mo)**: Up to 100 invoices chased/mo. Custom brand-voice tuning, direct debtor query triage.

## UK Legal & Compliance Guardrails (Non-Negotiable)

1. **PECR Corporate Subscriber Rule**:
   - Only send cold B2B emails to **Limited Companies (Ltd)**, **PLCs**, and **LLPs** under legitimate interest.
   - **DO NOT** cold email sole traders or partnerships without prior opt-in consent (PECR Reg 22 treats them as individual consumers).
2. **Clear Opt-Out**:
   - Every outreach email must include a clear, frictionless unsubscribe / opt-out statement (e.g., *"If you'd rather I not email again, just let me know and I'll remove you immediately."*).
3. **Regulatory Posture**:
   - Invoice Rescue is **NOT** a debt collection agency and is not FCA-regulated. Do not use debt collection agency terminology (e.g., "bailiffs", "debt recovery enforcement", "legal proceedings threatened"). Frame as credit control, cash flow recovery, and accounts receivables management.

## Tone & Voice Standards

- **Direct & Peer-to-Peer**: Written from founder/operator (Tibor) to business owner/director.
- **Concise**: Under 120 words. No lengthy corporate preambles.
- **Problem-Centric**: Acknowledge that service business founders hate awkward debtor conversations that strain client relationships.
- **CTA**: Low friction — offer a free, no-obligation aged debtor audit or review, rather than demanding a 30-minute sales call.
