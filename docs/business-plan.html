The deliverable

Hybrid framing — output you can point to, tied to the outcome they actually want:

Every overdue invoice chased within 24 hours of falling due, with escalating sequences sent in the client's own name — designed to get invoices paid 2–3 weeks sooner — plus a Friday cash report showing exactly what's been paid, promised, and escalating next.

The AI workflow — 5 steps, 80%+ AI-delivered

01

Connect

Client connects Xero/QuickBooks or sends a CSV. Onboarding takes under a day.

02

Detect

The system flags every invoice as it crosses due date — no manual review needed to spot what's overdue.

03

Draft

AI drafts the next message in the escalation sequence — reminder, firm follow-up, or formal notice with statutory interest calculated — tone-matched to the client's brand.

04

Review & send

You review each draft (roughly 5 minutes per client per day at steady state) and send in the client's name.

05

Report

An auto-generated Friday report goes to every client: paid, promised, escalating.

FINDING 05

The gap — where you win

Three forces line up: (1) a proven, quantified problem — 86 hours/year and £17K–£22K per business in unpaid invoices, per the Small Business Commissioner; (2) a regulatory tailwind pushing the topic into founder conversations right now via the Commercial Payments Bill; (3) an empty middle tier — Tier 1 agencies are priced for larger ledgers, Tier 2 marketplace freelancers have no software layer or consistency, and Tier 3 SaaS tools require the business owner to still do the chasing themselves. A single AI-assisted operator, personally branded, sitting between marketplace-cheap and agency-expensive, with software already built, is the open lane.

2–4 calls held, samples and proposals go out

  • Hold 2–4 discovery calls using the rehearsed framework

Week 4 · Iterate and push for close

Follow up, refine, close what's ready

  • 3-touch follow-up sequence on every outstanding proposal
  • Refine the pitch from whatever objections came up

  • If not yet closed: still on pace — this is the normal shape of month one
