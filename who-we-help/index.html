Who We Help

Built for the people who hate chasing money

If you're brilliant at your craft but terrible at chasing invoices, you're exactly who we built this for. Here are the people we work with most.

Freelance Designers

You pour weeks into a brand identity or website design, deliver pixel-perfect work, and then wait months for payment. Chasing feels at odds with the creative relationship you've built — so you don't. We handle it professionally, so you can focus on the next project.

IT Contractors

You've completed a contract, but the agency or end client is dragging their feet on payment. You're already on the next engagement and don't have time to chase. We step in as your professional representative and get the overdue invoices cleared.

Web Developers

You launched the site, handed over the credentials, and now the client has gone quiet. The final milestone payment never came. It's a frustratingly common pattern — and one we see (and resolve) regularly.

Photographers & Videographers

You shot the event, edited the footage, delivered the gallery — and now the client is avoiding your emails. Wedding and corporate photography invoices can sit unpaid for months. We follow up firmly and consistently until the balance is settled.

Tradespeople & Builders

You've done the physical work — the kitchen's fitted, the extension is built, the electrics are wired — but the customer is slow-paying or disputing. Chasing in person feels confrontational. We handle it remotely, professionally, and persistently.

Marketing Consultants

You've run the campaign, delivered the report, generated leads — but the retainer invoice is three months overdue. Marketing services are often seen as "soft" and easy to deprioritise. We make sure your work is valued and your invoice gets the attention it deserves.

The common thread

Every person we help is brilliant at what they do, but hates the awkward, energy-draining process of chasing money. They've usually tried once or twice, got nowhere, and are now stuck between writing it off and escalating. That's exactly where we come in — as your professional representative, we pick up the conversation and see it through. Learn more about how our process works or see our transparent pricing.
