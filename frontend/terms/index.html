The service

Invoice Rescue chases overdue invoices on a client's behalf, in the client's name, using an escalating sequence of reminder, firm follow-up, and formal notice — including statutory interest calculations where applicable under the Late Payment of Commercial Debts (Interest) Act 1998. Every message is reviewed by a human before it's sent.

Invoice Rescue is a credit-control service. We are not a debt collection agency, not solicitors, and not regulated by the FCA.

Plans

  • Foundation — £349/month, no long contract, up to 15 active invoices.
  • Engine — £649/month, 3-month minimum, up to 40 active invoices.
  • Operator — £1,250/month, 3-month minimum, unlimited invoices.

Prices exclude VAT where applicable. We can change plan pricing with notice before your next billing cycle.

Your responsibilities

  • The invoice and debtor data you give us is accurate and it's your invoice to chase.
  • You have a lawful basis to pursue the debt and to have us contact the debtor on your behalf.
  • You review escalation drafts promptly where our process asks for your input.

Cancellation

Foundation can be cancelled at any time. Engine and Operator have a 3-month minimum term, then roll month-to-month until cancelled. Cancel by emailing hello@invoicerescue.co.uk.
