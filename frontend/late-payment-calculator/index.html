How the figure is worked out

      
    
        
  • Interest starts the day after the payment due date. If your contract set no date, payment is late 30 days after the customer gets the invoice, or 30 days after you deliver the goods or service if that is later.
    • 
        
  • The rate is fixed for the whole debt by when it went overdue: the base rate on 31 December for debts overdue January to June, and on 30 June for debts overdue July to December.
    • 
        
  • Interest is simple interest: invoice total (including VAT) × annual rate × days late ÷ 365.
    • 
        
  • Fixed compensation per invoice: £40 under £1,000, £70 from £1,000 to £9,999.99, £100 at £10,000 or more.
    • 
        
  • It doesn't apply to consumers, to invoices under genuine dispute, or where your contract sets its own interest rate for late payment.
